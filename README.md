## ❓ The Problem

Managing certificates in Windows is a pain. The built-in MMC snap‑in is clunky, slow, and requires navigating through multiple menus. Finding a specific certificate, viewing its details, or exporting it to the right format takes too many clicks.

This tool gives you a clean, simple GUI to manage all your Windows certificates in one place.

---

## ⚡ What This Tool Does

- **Browse all certificate stores** – Personal, Root, CA, and more
- **View detailed certificate info** – issuer, expiry, thumbprint, subject, serial number
- **Export certificates** – to DER, PEM, and PKCS#12 formats
- **Search and filter** – quickly find specific certificates
- **Certificate Trust List (CTL) viewer** – view trust lists directly
- **Portable** – no installation required
